Academic results: In process

M.Sc. in Economics' Thesis

“Urban Sprawl in Belgium: How Does Urban Sprawl Affect Local Government Expenditure Levels”

Examine the impact of urban sprawl on municipal public expenditure in Belgium using a panel of 581 municipalities over 2003–2013. Drawing on data from Statbel, the Flemish Agency for Interior Affairs, and the OECD.Using a urban dispersion indicator based on built-up area per capita and estimated OLS and 2SLS models with regional and time fixed effects.

Academic result: Final grade 14/20.
